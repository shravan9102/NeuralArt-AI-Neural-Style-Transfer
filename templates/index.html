Frequently Asked Questions

Some helpful information before generating your artwork.

Neural style transfer uses a neural network to combine the content of one image with the visual characteristics of another, such as colors, patterns, and textures.

Alpha controls the balance between stylized features and original content features. A value of 0 keeps the content features, while 1 uses the full stylized representation.

The interface accepts PNG, JPG, and JPEG images. Each upload is limited to 16 MB in this interface. Your Flask backend should enforce the same limit.

Processing time depends on your computer, whether a compatible GPU is available, and the image dimensions. CPU processing may take longer than GPU processing.
